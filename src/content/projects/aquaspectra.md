## The project

[AquaMesh](https://aquamesh.ai/company.html) started as sensor research at UC San Diego and now makes sensors and software for industrial water. Its first [AquaSpectra](https://aquamesh.ai/#aquaspectra) sensor was built in 2025 for Scripps Institution of Oceanography: a broad-spectrum optical instrument that reads many things about the water from one rugged device.

## My part

I'm helping AquaMesh get the sensor ready for research use. That means the remaining software interface, the calibration workflow and tuning the sensor across marine conditions. Day to day, I review the probe and hub firmware and the front-end software, run calibration and test workflows, look at how the sensor behaves, and write up what I find and what I'd change.
